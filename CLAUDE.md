## Product development workflow <!-- luka-product-workflow -->

- Product context: `docs/product/context.md`
- Signals: `docs/product/signals.md`
- Metrics: `docs/product/metrics.md`
- Feature briefs: `docs/product/features/`
- Decision records: `docs/product/decisions/`

For medium- or high-risk changes affecting users, workflows, forms, data,
navigation, permissions, notifications, payments, automations, or AI behavior:

1. Treat the request as a signal or proposed solution.
2. Separate evidence from assumptions.
3. Identify the user, context, problem, risk, hypothesis, and success measure.
4. Compare viable approaches before selecting one.
5. Create or update a feature brief.
6. Implement the smallest coherent solution.
7. Verify acceptance criteria and define production follow-up.

Use `/product-workflow:product-workflow` explicitly when automatic activation
does not occur. Low-risk copy, cosmetic, and obvious defect fixes may proceed
directly with verification.
